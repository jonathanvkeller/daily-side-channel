## found object · a cassette label where being a musician isn't enough

Oslo label Breton Cassette uses an unusually productive membership rule: released artists must be both musicians and visual artists.

The catalog's identity does not need to come from one sound.

Coherence comes from a shared creative condition.

## found object · the record is a place assembled from scraps

*veil of discontinuity* is assembled from field recordings, instrumental experiments, environmental sound, and electroacoustic material.

The useful workflow:

collect → listen → notice relationships → assemble

Sometimes the seam between sources is the composition.

## studio fuel · a card game with a manifesto instead of rules

A collage-based card game suggests a design distinction.

Rules ask:

> what actions are legal?

A manifesto asks:

> what kind of behavior are we trying to produce together?

Before mechanics, try five sentences beginning:

> In this game, we believe…

## rabbit hole · every finite perfect-information game is already decided

Any finite two-player perfect-information game is in one of three states:

- player one can force a win
- player two can force a win
- both can force at least a draw

The philosophical trapdoor:

> determined does not mean known.

## crosswire · cassette labels × game design

Define membership instead of output.

Do not require every work to look the same. Define the condition under which something belongs.

Identity can come from rules rather than surface consistency.

## 20 minutes · write the manifesto before the game

Write exactly five lines beginning:

> In this game, we believe…

Each should describe an experience, not a setting.

Pick one line and invent the smallest possible mechanic that makes violating it difficult.
