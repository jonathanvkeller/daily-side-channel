## FOUND OBJECT · The record that gets less identical with every play

Christian Marclay's 1985 Record Without a Cover was distributed without protective packaging. Scratches and wear become part of the sound. MoMA holds an original edition. Two owners can start with the same recording and gradually acquire different works. The collector's usual enemy becomes a collaborator.

[MoMA object](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/184035) · [Background](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_Without_a_Cover)

## STUDIO FUEL · Compose a deteriorating loop

Record a short guitar phrase. Duplicate it four times in GarageBand. In each successive copy, remove one additional attack or shorten one sustained note. Keep the timing grid unchanged. The missing material accumulates rather than resetting. Listen for the point at which the damage becomes the rhythm. This is a reversible digital experiment, not a suggestion to damage records or equipment.

## RABBIT HOLE · A drawing that is also a number

John Conway's Hackenbush is played by cutting colored edges connected to a ground line. Blue belongs to one player, red to the other. Anything disconnected from the ground disappears. A player unable to move loses.

A single blue edge has value 1. A red edge atop a blue edge has value 1/2: Blue can remove everything, leaving zero; Red can remove the upper edge, leaving Blue with 1. In Conway's established theory, the simplest number between these options is 1/2. These are game values, not winning probabilities. The mathematics leads into surreal numbers, a system encompassing ordinary real numbers and much more.

[Whitman College mathematical introduction](https://www.whitman.edu/Documents/Academics/Mathematics/Grimm.pdf)

## CROSSWIRE · The notebook with a second author

A late-fifteenth-century Danish manuscript, AM 76 8vo, contains six manicules: little pointing hands drawn in the margins. The University of Copenhagen examined them in September 2025. The manuscript contains 58 texts, including the only surviving Old Danish manuscript copy of Lucidarius.

[See the six hands](https://manuscript.ku.dk/motm/pointing-out-manicules)

Combine marginalia with solo roleplaying. Keep two layers in your campaign journal: the original character's observations and the annotations of a later reader. Give the second reader a different profession and motive. They may underline a trivial detail or dispute a conclusion, but cannot change the original text. Their attention becomes an oracle for your next session.

## 20 MINUTES · Draw a game you can destroy

Spend five minutes drawing a grounded tree with twelve red and blue edges. Spend five playing both sides of Hackenbush, alternating turns and crossing out disconnected branches. Spend five replaying with a different opening move. Spend the final five comparing the two surviving intermediate drawings and inventing a fictional institution whose dependencies resemble the tree.

The useful question: which apparently minor connection holds the whole thing together?
