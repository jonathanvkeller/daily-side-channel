## found object · artifact versus archive

A useful publishing split:

print = artifact  
web = archive

The physical object can be scarce, designed, folded, xeroxed, and handled without forcing the writing itself to disappear.

## studio fuel · steal the surface of an object

Instead of depicting an object, make the object participate in representation.

Use its rubbing, outline, shadow, dimensions, wear, stain, packaging, or impression.

## rabbit hole · Riso isn't really a printer

Risograph work is built from separate ink layers. Overlap becomes an additional visual state.

Stealable mechanism:

> design images by deciding what each layer knows.

Try architecture / organisms / damage as three independent plates. Combine only at the end.

## found object · wrong-tool art

Tetra Pak cartons can become intaglio plates because their laminate surface can be scratched and inked.

The better question is not "what trash can I make art from?"

It is:

> what property was this material engineered to have, and how can I misuse that property?

## crosswire · riso × solo RPG design

Generate independent layers:

PLACE: river crossing  
PRESSURE: winter food shortage  
PAST: failed pilgrimage

Then force yourself to explain the overlap.

The world emerges from registration between layers.

## 20 minutes · make a three-plate monster

Make three layers:

BODY  
WORLD  
WRONG

Do not view the composite until the end. Do not fix the collisions.
