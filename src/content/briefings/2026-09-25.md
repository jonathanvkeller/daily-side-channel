## the signal · mathematicians built the graph sandwich

A difficult random graph model can be trapped between two easier ones and inherit useful properties from them.

Broad strategy:

> don't understand the hard object directly. trap it between objects you already understand.

## found object · acoustic instruments pretending they're modular synthesizers

A cello-and-violin duo borrows electronic *logic* rather than electronic sound.

Sequencing. Gating. Feedback. Clock division. Envelopes.

That is a richer form of influence than timbral imitation.

## found object · twelve people bind the same book

Give multiple binders the same unbound text and the result demonstrates:

identical content + different physical interpretation = different object

Binding becomes criticism.

## rabbit hole · microhistory

Microhistory studies something tiny enough that larger systems leak through it.

One miller can reveal literacy, authority, religion, printing, class, and oral culture.

Tiny evidence → big structure.

## crosswire · microhistory × solo RPGs

Do not generate a kingdom. Generate one nobody.

"Marta sells candles outside the cathedral."

Then ask where her wax comes from, who controls her stall, who taught her numbers, and why prices doubled.

One candle seller forces the world into existence.

## 20 minutes · build a world from one receipt

Make a fake medieval receipt with five mundane items.

Interrogate each line until the surrounding world becomes unavoidable.
