## the signal · local print systems

make → duplicate → trade → meet → archive

## found object · medieval literature survived because somebody needed a notebook cover

Medieval manuscript fragments sometimes survive because later people cut them up and reused them as covers for administrative books.

The paradox:

destruction → reuse → preservation

A sacred or literary text becomes office supplies, then historical evidence.

## rabbit hole · reverse mathematics

Normal mathematics asks:

axioms → theorem

Reverse mathematics asks:

theorem → which axioms were actually necessary?

Strip assumptions away until one more removal breaks the result.

## studio fuel · build with salvage, not texture

Do not use an abandoned page as background texture.

Give it a new physical job: hinge, pocket, tab, mask, binding strip, window.

## crosswire · reverse mathematics × game design

Start with the experience you want, then remove mechanics one by one.

If the experience survives, the mechanic stays dead.

What remains is the minimum ruleset capable of producing the experience.

## 20 minutes · murder one mechanic

Write a game's intended experience in one sentence. List every mechanic. Cross them out until deleting the next one would break the experience.
