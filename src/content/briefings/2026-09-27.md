## found object · 30 tapes and a van

At thirty copies, physical media stops behaving like manufacturing and starts behaving like correspondence.

Distribution is still social. You can carry the edition to shows, mail it yourself, and plausibly know where many copies went.

Scale changes cultural geometry.

## rabbit hole · two twists equal zero twists

Tangloids demonstrate a strange fact related to spinors.

A 360° rotation tangles a three-string system in a way that cannot be removed without undoing the rotation.

At 720°, the system can be untangled without reversing the rotation.

In the relevant topology:

360° ≠ 0  
720° = 0

## found object · the manuscript comparison machine

The better manuscript question is not:

> what did a medieval manuscript look like?

It is:

> how differently could people make the same book?

## studio fuel · commit before you know whether it's good

Digital work encourages:

make → inspect → undo → adjust

A constrained process can encourage:

make → commit → respond

The point is not artificial hardship. It is delaying evaluation until interesting accidents have accumulated.

## crosswire · manuscript copies × music transmission

Teach a song through people instead of recordings.

Misremembered details become arrangement decisions.

The question becomes:

> what survived transmission strongly enough to remain the song?

## 20 minutes · make a three-string spinor

Build a crude Tangloid from cardboard and string. Fight with the 720° case before looking up the solution.
